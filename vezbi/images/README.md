# Supplemental illustration

`cezve.png` is an original AI-generated illustration of a ѓезве, produced with
the built-in imagegen tool on 10 October 2026. It is **not** an ARASAAC asset;
the kitchen theme credits it separately. The other theme pictures are embedded
ARASAAC pictograms, credited to Sergio Palao / Government of Aragon under
CC BY-NC-SA, with their IDs in `../themes-manifest.json`.

The theme builder packs this original into `../temi.js` at up to 300 × 300 px;
the original stays here for regeneration. The page makes no image-service call.

Generation prompt:

> Create one simple educational vocabulary pictogram of a traditional Balkan
> Turkish coffee pot, called cezve or ѓезве. Isolated single copper pot: small
> tapered cylindrical body wider at base, open top with visible oval rim and
> small pouring lip, one long dark brown handle extending diagonally to upper
> right. Bold clean black outlines, flat warm copper fill, minimal detail,
> child-friendly recognisable object. Straightforward side three-quarter view,
> generous padding. Plain white background. No text, no logo, no letters, no
> cup, no saucer, no steam, no person. This is an original supplemental icon
> for a children's Macedonian vocabulary app.
